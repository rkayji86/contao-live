Step 1: Account Registration with GutscheinWerft

Before integrating vouchers into Booking Experts, each user needs a valid account with GutscheinWerft. Currently, the registration is handled manually.

How to Register:

  1. Contact GutscheinWerft: Reach out via email or the official contact form to request an account.
  2. Await Confirmation: The GutscheinWerft team will process your request and create an account.
  3. Receive Login Details: You will receive an email with your credentials and further instructions.

Note: A self-registration process is currently under development. In the future, users will be able to sign up directly on the GutscheinWerft website.

Step 2: Webhook Setup in GutscheinWerft Backend

After receiving their account, the user logs into the GutscheinWerft backend, navigates to Settings > Webhooks, and clicks "Webhook" to begin configuration.

Step 3: Adding a New Webhook

Once you are in the webhook management section, you need to create a new webhook for your Booking Experts integration. Click on the "Neuen Webhook hinzufügen" button to start setting up a new webhook configuration.

Step 4: Selecting the Webhook Trigger

In the webhook configuration form, you need to specify when the webhook should be triggered. Look for the "Auslösen wenn" (Trigger when) section and select the appropriate trigger event that will activate the webhook for your Booking Experts integration.

Step 5: Selecting the Target System

In the webhook configuration form, proceed to the section labeled "Angebundenes System" (Connected System). Here, you must select "BookingExperts" as the system that should receive the webhook event. This ensures all voucher-related events are sent directly to your Booking Experts installation.

Step 6: Saving the Webhook Configuration

After completing all necessary fields—choosing the trigger event and selecting "BookingExperts" as the connected system—finalize your webhook setup. Click the "Speichern" (Save) button at the bottom of the configuration form. Your new webhook will now appear in the list and will be active for communicating with Booking Experts. You will need the "Shopnummer" and "Key" later for setting up BookingExperts Configuration.

Configure your BookingExperts system to receive and process voucher data from GutscheinWerft

Step 7: Accessing the App Store

To begin, log in to your BookingExperts system and navigate to the App Store.

Step 8: Searching for GutscheinWERFT

Use the search bar at the top to search for 'GutscheinWERFT'.

Step 9: Installing the GutscheinWERFT App

Click on the 'GutscheinWERFT' app from the search results, then click the 'Install' button on its details page.

Step 10: Review Permissions and Install

Review the required permissions, select the correct administration at the bottom of the page, and click 'Install' again.

Step 11: Enter GutscheinWERFT Credentials

A new page will open. Enter the credentials from the webhook you created in the GutscheinWerft backend. You can find them again by clicking on the webhook in GutscheinWerft.

Step 12: Submit Credentials

After entering the credentials, click the 'Submit' button to save and verify the connection.

Step 13: Confirm Connection

A success page will confirm the connection. Click 'Back to BookingExperts' to return to your dashboard.

Step 14: Return to the App Store

You are now back at the BookingExperts Dashboard. Click again on 'AppStore' to view your installed apps.

Step 15: Verify Installation

Verify the installation by confirming that 'GutscheinWERFT' now appears at the top of your installed apps list.

Integration Complete

You have now successfully integrated GutscheinWERFT with your BookingExperts system. From this point forward, you can begin selling vouchers directly to your customers through your VoucherShop, and your guests can redeem them as a payment method during the online booking process.
